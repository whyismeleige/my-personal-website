## Blue Screen of Death

Everyone who has used windows, has at some point faced this problem.

**Blue screen of death**, in middle of your work, when you require your PC to work the most and I was facing this issue with windows for a long time during 2023 to 2024 and it was extremely annoying. You have copilot and AI pushed down your throat by everything in windows. One random update just breaks down your PC for no reason.

As I was learning programming, it was becoming cumbersome to handle windows anymore. So, I did what any sane person would do, I switched to a Penguin.

![An anime character sitting with their arm around Tux, the Linux penguin](neon-linux.jpeg)

## The Leap of Faith

At first, I was pretty skeptical and scared like I din’t want to accidentally remove anything important by deleting windows or else my father would beat my ass, if I damage the PC in any sort of way.

I went with Linux Mint as my first choice because many people online suggested it was the best possible distro among the thousands of other Linux distros for beginners to comfortably use.

I started using the terminal a lot, came across various tools such as Tmux ( what a great tool, like Tmux is the Real G ), etc.

I felt, I was in Heaven.

There is no bloatware anymore, no one is shoving AI down my throat, and I felt my productivity skyrocket and the fun fact this is entirely free, like really free and it is freaking awesome of how good your PC can feel you, everything is at your fingertips.

![Kung Fu Panda meme — "The reasons why Windows is better than Linux" / "It's blank"](linux-memes.jpeg)

## Linux Mint was Not Enough

I went totally into the Linux rabbit hole, ricing and doing all sorts of stuff which was impossible to do previously in Windows. Linux was in fact, boosting my productivity to another level that i could not have imagined before.

But, there was still a catch, there were certain personal configurations, I could not do, due to the lack of customization options in Linux Mint.

So, one of my friend suggested to use Arch Linux, I had heard about Arch Linux before and of course with all the memes around Arch users, so I thought of giving it a shot and I went through the arduous path of installing Arch Linux.

![Meme of a man sobbing beside a woman captioned "No, I will not watch you install Arch Linux"](arch-meme-2.png)

## Installing Arch is "complicated"

Before anyone points out, I did not use archinstall and did my installation via the hardcore manual way as our god father Linus Torvalds intended.

Well, installing arch is one hell of a thing in itself to do. With managing all the partitions and properly configuring your keys, well it is a task but I completed in a day and I tell you. It is not that complicated as many people claim it to be.

![A wojak in an Arch Linux t-shirt standing in front of the Arch logo](arch-btw.jpeg)

## I use Arch btw

It has already been a year since I started using Arch along with a tiling window manager like Hyprland and honestly it is one of the best experiences of using a PC to its maximum efficiency, i have ever done.

I am not that deep into ricing arch but it gives me enough capability and power over my PC to control everything and increase my productivity to the next level and somehow the more i go deeper into this, the better arch linux feels to me and I forgot one thing to say that **I use Arch btw**
